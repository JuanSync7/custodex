# Document type: register

A register is the live list. The reader's contract is: *show me what is
outstanding, right now.* Its value is entirely in being current — a stale
register is worse than none, because it is trusted.

Guidance:

- Every row carries an owner, a status and a date. A row with no owner is not
  tracked, it is merely written down.
- Grade entries with a marker taken from the set already in use in this document,
  spelled exactly as it appears there. Do not coin a synonym, and do not replace
  a marker with a prose hedge ("mostly working").
- Quantify with denominators: "11 of 11 audited", never a bare "healthy". A
  summary line without its denominator is the shape in which a register lies.
- Close rows; never silently delete them. A closed row with its outcome is
  evidence; a missing row is a gap the reader cannot see.
- Never call an item accepted, mitigated or resolved unless the entry names who
  accepted it, what compensates for it, and when it is next reviewed. If any of
  the three is missing, it is open and unowned — say so, and do not invent the
  missing part.

## Not its job

Narrating how the list got this way, or explaining any single entry at length.
Point at the document that owns the detail.

## A note on region authority

A register's value is that its rows are maintained in place, and rows carry
owners, dates and outcomes that no code surface contains. Only author a region
whose rows genuinely derive from the surface; the judgement columns belong in a
`human` region. Never invent an owner, a date, or a status you cannot see.
